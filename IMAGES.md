## Editorial notes

- The three triptych panels are AI-enhanced editorial portraits. They are used as atmosphere and storytelling, not as documentary evidence.
- The temple portrait, the Himalayan portrait, the selfies, and the café photograph are the real anchors for biography and trust.
- The Himalayan portrait is below full-screen desktop hero resolution, so the home page uses a type-led hero with the photograph as a portrait panel.
- The Open Graph card is generated from the triptych at 1200 × 630 (`/public/og/chef-mani-bista.jpg`).
- App icons are generated from `public/brand/favicon.svg` at 180, 192, and 512 px.
